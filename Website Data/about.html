About

Builder. Solver.
Perpetual debugger.

I'm a Computer Science undergrad at ICLES' Motilal Jhunjhunwala College, Vashi (University of Mumbai), obsessively grinding DSA on GeeksforGeeks while building real-world AI-powered applications.

My approach is simple: submit, read the error, fix exactly what broke, repeat. That loop has gotten me through Tarjan's algorithm, Dijkstra variants, 3D DP, and a particularly spicy Huffman encoding tie-breaker at 2 AM.

Outside of code, I serve as a GeeksforGeeks Campus Mantri — organizing tech programs, creating content, and evangelizing competitive programming on campus. I also run a small study group with a shared notes and solved-problem-set repository, and tinker with DIY electronics on the side.
